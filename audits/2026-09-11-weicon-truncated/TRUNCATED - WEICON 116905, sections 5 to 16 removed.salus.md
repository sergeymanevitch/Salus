182│4.1 Description of first aid measures
183│ Eye contact                            : Get medical attention immediately. Call a poison center or physician. Immediately
184│                                          flush eyes with plenty of water, occasionally lifting the upper and lower eyelids.
185│                                          Check for and remove any contact lenses. Continue to rinse for at least 10 minutes.

197│SECTION 4: First aid measures
198│ Inhalation                           : Get medical attention immediately. Call a poison center or physician. Remove
199│                                        victim to fresh air and keep at rest in a position comfortable for breathing. If it is
200│                                        suspected that fumes are still present, the rescuer should wear an appropriate mask
201│                                        or self-contained breathing apparatus. If not breathing, if breathing is irregular or if
202│                                        respiratory arrest occurs, provide artificial respiration or oxygen by trained personnel.
203│                                        It may be dangerous to the person providing aid to give mouth-to-mouth
204│                                        resuscitation. If unconscious, place in recovery position and get medical attention
205│                                        immediately. Maintain an open airway. Loosen tight clothing such as a collar, tie,
206│                                        belt or waistband.
207│ Skin contact                         : Get medical attention immediately. Call a poison center or physician. Wash skin
208│                                        thoroughly with soap and water or use recognized skin cleanser. Remove
209│                                        contaminated clothing and shoes. Wash contaminated clothing thoroughly with
210│                                        water before removing it, or wear gloves. Continue to rinse for at least 10 minutes.
211│                                        Chemical burns must be treated promptly by a physician. In the event of any
212│                                        complaints or symptoms, avoid further exposure. Wash clothing before reuse.
213│                                        Clean shoes thoroughly before reuse.
214│ Ingestion                            : Get medical attention immediately. Call a poison center or physician. Wash out
215│                                        mouth with water. Remove dentures if any. If material has been swallowed and the
216│                                        exposed person is conscious, give small quantities of water to drink. Stop if the
217│                                        exposed person feels sick as vomiting may be dangerous. Do not induce vomiting
218│                                        unless directed to do so by medical personnel. If vomiting occurs, the head should
219│                                        be kept low so that vomit does not enter the lungs. Chemical burns must be treated
220│                                        promptly by a physician. Never give anything by mouth to an unconscious person.
221│                                        If unconscious, place in recovery position and get medical attention immediately.
222│                                        Maintain an open airway. Loosen tight clothing such as a collar, tie, belt or
223│                                        waistband.
224│ Protection of first-aiders           : No action shall be taken involving any personal risk or without suitable training. If it
225│                                        is suspected that fumes are still present, the rescuer should wear an appropriate
226│                                        mask or self-contained breathing apparatus. It may be dangerous to the person
227│                                        providing aid to give mouth-to-mouth resuscitation. Wash contaminated clothing
228│                                        thoroughly with water before removing it, or wear gloves.
