152│ 4. First-aid Measures
153│4.1    Description of First Aid Measures
154│       AFTER INHALATION: Give oxygen or artificial respiration if needed. Remove person to fresh air. If signs/symptoms
155│       continue, get medical attention.
156│       AFTER SKIN CONTACT: In case of contact, immediately flush skin with plenty of water for at least 15 minutes while
157│       removing contaminated clothing and shoes. If skin irritation persists, call a physician.
158│       AFTER EYE CONTACT: Rinse thoroughly with plenty of water for at least 15 minutes and consult a physician.
159│       AFTER INGESTION: Do NOT induce vomiting. Never give anything by mouth to an unconscious person. If swallowed, call
160│       a poison control centre or doctor immediately.
161│4.2    Most important symptoms and effects, both acute and delayed
162│       Harmful if swallowed. Irritating to eyes and skin. Risk of serious damage to the lungs (by aspiration). Vapours may cause
163│       drowsiness and dizziness.
164│4.3    Indication of any immediate medical attention and special treatment needed
165│
166│       No information available on clinical testing and medical monitoring. Specific toxicological information on substances, if
167│       available, can be found in section 11.
168│       When symptoms persist or in all cases of doubt seek medical advice.

170│ 5. Fire-fighting Measures
171│5.1   Extinguishing Media:
172│      Carbon Dioxide, Dry Chemical, Foam, Water Fog
173│      UNUSUAL FIRE AND EXPLOSION HAZARDS: Flammable liquid. Vapours are heavier than air and may spread along
174│      floors. Vapours may form explosive mixtures with air. Vapors may travel to areas away from work site before igniting/flashing
175│      back to vapor source. Provide adequate ventilation. Prevent the creation of flammable or explosive concentrations of vapour
176│      in air and avoid vapour concentration higher than the occupational exposure limits. Keep away from heat/sparks/open
177│      flames/hot surfaces. - No smoking. Electrical installations / working materials must comply with the technological safety
178│      standards. Wear shoes with conductive soles.

292│      Personal Protection
293│       RESPIRATORY PROTECTION: In order to avoid inhalation of spray-mist and sanding dust, all spraying and sanding must
294│       be done wearing adequate respirator. Use only with ventilation to keep levels below exposure guidelines reported in this
295│       document. User should test and monitor exposure levels to ensure all personnel are below guidelines. If not sure, or not able
296│       to monitor, use State or federally approved supplied air respirator. For silica containing coatings in a liquid state, and/or if no

298│       EYE PROTECTION: Ensure that eyewash stations and safety showers are close to the workstation location. Safety glasses
299│       with side-shields.

301│       breakthrough. Impervious gloves. Request information on glove permeation properties from the glove supplier. Lightweight
302│       protective clothing
303│       OTHER PROTECTIVE EQUIPMENT: No Information
304│       ENGINEERING CONTROLS: Avoid contact with skin, eyes and clothing. Ensure adequate ventilation, especially in confined
