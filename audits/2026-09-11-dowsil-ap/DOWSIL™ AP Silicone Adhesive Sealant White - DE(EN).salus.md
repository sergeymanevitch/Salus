 175│SECTION 4: FIRST AID MEASURES
 176│4.1 Description of first aid measures
 177│General advice:
 178│First Aid responders should pay attention to self-protection and use the recommended protective
 179│clothing (chemical resistant gloves, splash protection). If potential for exposure exists refer to Section
 180│8 for specific personal protective equipment.
 181│
 182│Inhalation: Move person to fresh air and keep comfortable for breathing. If not breathing, give
 183│artificial respiration; if by mouth to mouth use rescuer protection (pocket mask, etc). If breathing is
 184│difficult, oxygen should be administered by qualified personnel. Call a physician or transport to a
 185│medical facility.
 186│
 187│Skin contact: Remove material from skin immediately by washing with soap and plenty of water.
 188│Remove contaminated clothing and shoes while washing. Seek medical attention if irritation or rash
 189│occurs. Wash clothing before reuse. Discard items which cannot be decontaminated, including leather
 190│articles such as shoes, belts and watchbands.
 191│
 192│Eye contact: Flush eyes thoroughly with water for several minutes. Remove contact lenses after the
 193│initial 1-2 minutes and continue flushing for several additional minutes. If effects occur, consult a
 194│physician, preferably an ophthalmologist.
 195│
 196│Ingestion: Rinse mouth with water. No emergency medical treatment necessary.
 197│
 198│4.2 Most important symptoms and effects, both acute and delayed:
 199│Aside from the information found under Description of first aid measures (above) and Indication of
 200│immediate medical attention and special treatment needed (below), any additional important
 201│symptoms and effects are described in Section 11: Toxicology Information.
 202│
 203│4.3 Indication of any immediate medical attention and special treatment needed
 204│Notes to physician: No specific antidote. Treatment of exposure should be directed at the control of
 205│symptoms and the clinical condition of the patient.

 473│Individual protection measures
 474│        Eye/face protection: Use safety glasses (with side shields). Safety glasses (with side shields)
 475│        should be consistent with EN 166 or equivalent.
 476│        Skin protection
 477│                 Hand protection: Use chemical resistant gloves classified under Standard EN374:
 478│                 Protective gloves against chemicals and micro-organisms. Examples of preferred
 479│                 glove barrier materials include: Butyl rubber. Natural rubber ("latex"). Neoprene.
 480│                 Nitrile/butadiene rubber ("nitrile" or "NBR"). Ethyl vinyl alcohol laminate ("EVAL").
 481│                 Polyvinyl chloride ("PVC" or "vinyl"). When prolonged or frequently repeated contact
 482│                 may occur, a glove with a protection class of 5 or higher (breakthrough time greater
 483│                 than 240 minutes according to EN 374) is recommended. When only brief contact is
 484│                 expected, a glove with a protection class of 3 or higher (breakthrough time greater
 485│                 than 60 minutes according to EN 374) is recommended. Glove thickness alone is not
 486│                 a good indicator of the level of protection a glove provides against a chemical
 487│                 substance as this level of protection is also highly dependent on the specific
 488│                 composition of the material that the glove is fabricated from. The thickness of the
 489│                 glove must, depending on model and type of material, generally be more than 0.35
 490│                 mm to offer sufficient protection for prolonged and frequent contact with the substance.
 491│                 As an exception to this general rule it is known that multilayer laminate gloves may
 492│                 offer prolonged protection at thicknesses less than 0.35 mm. Other glove materials
 493│                 with a thickness of less than 0.35 mm may offer sufficient protection when only brief
 494│                 contact is expected. NOTICE: The selection of a specific glove for a particular
 495│                 application and duration of use in a workplace should also take into account all
 496│                 relevant workplace factors such as, but not limited to: Other chemicals which may be
 497│                 handled, physical requirements (cut/puncture protection, dexterity, thermal protection),
 498│                 potential body reactions to glove materials, as well as the instructions/specifications
 499│                 provided by the glove supplier.
