150│ Eye contact                    : Immediately flush eyes with plenty of water, occasionally lifting the upper and lower
151│                                  eyelids. Check for and remove any contact lenses. Get medical attention if irritation

154│                                  Get medical attention if symptoms occur. In case of inhalation of decomposition
